# HVIA × Olist — Business Case

## Overview

This project analyzes the public **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset** to identify logistics-related business opportunities and propose an AI/ML solution.

## Key Findings

* Overall delivery delay rate: **6.44%**
* Delayed orders had a lower average review score (**2.26**) compared to on-time orders (**4.15**).
* Higher-freight orders showed higher observed delay rates:

  * Low Freight: **4.34%**
  * Medium Freight: **7.01%**
  * High Freight: **7.96%**
* Delivery performance varied significantly across customer states.

## Proposed Solution

### Predictive Delivery Risk Engine

A machine learning system that predicts the probability of an order being delayed using factors such as:

* Geographic information
* Freight value
* Product characteristics
* Order characteristics
* Historical delivery patterns

The goal is to identify potentially high-risk orders early and provide actionable signals to **Data/AI, Logistics, and Operations teams**.

### Future Extension

With carrier-level operational data, the system could be extended to support **carrier performance analysis and routing decisions**.

## Deliverables

* Data analysis notebook

## Disclaimer

This analysis is based on the public Olist dataset. The findings represent observed associations and should not be interpreted as confirmed information about Olist's current internal operations.
